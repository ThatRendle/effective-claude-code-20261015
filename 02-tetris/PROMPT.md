I want a playable Tetris game that runs in the browser.

It needs:

- a 10 × 20 board, the seven tetrominoes, and a preview of the next piece
- keyboard controls: move left and right, rotate, soft drop, and hard drop
- gravity, line clears, and a score

It should run with nothing more than Node.js and a browser.
